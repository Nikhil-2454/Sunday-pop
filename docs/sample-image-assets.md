# SUNDAY POP sample product images

The following image assets were generated as temporary visual concepts and cropped from one sample flavour-lineup image:

- `frontend/public/assets/products/cheese-burst/main.webp`
- `frontend/public/assets/products/butter-blast/main.webp`
- `frontend/public/assets/products/peri-peri/main.webp`
- Matching `thumbnail.webp` files in each flavour directory.
- `frontend/public/assets/home/sample-flavour-lineup.webp`

The existing frontend maps the sample product records `cheesy-pop`, `golden-butter`, and `peri-peri-blast` to their corresponding flavour images.

These are illustrative sample visuals, not approved packaging artwork. Do not use them as final label artwork or as evidence of ingredients, allergens, net quantity, FSSAI details, nutrition, shelf life, or other regulated product claims. Replace them with approved production photography and artwork before commercial launch.
